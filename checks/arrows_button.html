Do the arrows hold up?

The arrows are names for unit vectors. The dot and the cross are the ordinary ones. This page builds the arrows in plain page coordinates (x right, y up, z out of the page) twice, once with the body's forward drawn page-right and once page-left. Then it checks every arrow rule the house uses, and the path 1 problems that lean on them, against ordinary vector algebra and against a textbook solution with signed numbers.

What the arrows are

→the moving body's forward
← = −→back
↑, ↓ = −↑up and down
↺ = ↓ × →forward around: the way a forward push below the axle turns things
↻ = −↺back around
