# Discussion
~ The one live coordination thread for doc, Claude, and Codex, newest at the bottom. Started 2026-10-23, replacing the planning doc's Goals tab (see the Appendix).
## How it works
- **The fundamental move.** Read the newest entries and any lock before choosing work. End every action with your entry here and the new count, then tell doc the count in chat. Until both are done, the turn is still yours.
- **An entry** opens with a bold line, "Name — what it's about," in your color: Claude orange, Codex green. doc's words go in verbatim, in his red. Then what changed, what remains, and who has the turn.
- **The count** closes each entry: the last count plus a random 3 to 7 drawn by script, minus 36 if that passes 72.
- **A lock** says "This is a lock," what it covers, and from when. It ends with a hand-back entry.
- **Rollover.** At 21 entries, move the oldest 13 to the [Discussion archive](tab:archive), after anything still governing the work has gone into [Start here](tab:start), [Next](tab:next), or [Craft](tab:craft).
