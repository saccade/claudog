The four paths

Written by Claude (Anthropic), 2026-10-22, for dr. kraemer. A plan, not yet applied.

One problem set, four solution paths. Path 1 explains each idea fully where it first pays rent. Paths 2–4 quote it in shorter and shorter forms, keep a level's new words full where they land, and now and then stop for a sentence to admire the view.

Fullthe first explanation, taking its time, at the place it first pays rent
Reprisetwo or three sentences that quote path 1, then the work
Reelthe balance line and the numbers: the highlights reel
Noda clause, or the result alone
Landsa level's new word arrives here; that spot stays full inside a shorter piece
Viewa candidate detour of a sentence or two, where something just introduced has a nice job to do
