Each staff is one term, read left to right through a path's solutions. A note is a use. A red stem is a gloss, a sentence that says what the term is. A shaded stretch is a gap longer than the dial, and the mark where the term comes back shows whether it came back with a nod or cold.

The distance is the path as a reader meets it, solutions and then the ending, in characters, with the statements left out. Path 1's solutions run about 18 thousand characters, so 4 thousand is roughly three problems.

A gloss is found by its wording (call, is the, that's the, means, read, we'll, or a colon right after the term), so it misses some and catches some that aren't. Hover a mark for its sentence. Arrow counts include every arrow on the page, so their staves are dense by nature.
