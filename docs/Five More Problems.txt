FIVE MORE PROBLEMS
==================

Problem statements only. Figures not reproduced; assumed geometry
is stated.

1. The ladder that slides
-------------------------

    A ladder of length L = 3.0 m leans against a frictionless
    wall, its foot on a frictionless floor. At one instant the
    foot is 1.8 m from the wall, the top is 2.4 m up the wall, and
    the foot is sliding away from the wall at 0.40 m/s.
    (a) How fast is the top moving, and how fast is the ladder's
        angle changing?
    (b) Find the velocity of the ladder's centre. Show that the
        centre moves on a circle, and find it.
    (c) Find the point that is momentarily at rest, and show that
        every point of the ladder moves as if the ladder were
        turning about it.

    (This version looks at the ladder with the wall on the left.)

2. The rod on the ice
---------------------

    A uniform rod of mass m = 0.60 kg and length L = 1.2 m lies
    at rest on frictionless ice. Seen from above it lies north to
    south. A puck moving east strikes its north end, delivering a
    horizontal impulse J = 1.8 N s, due east, in a very short
    time. The rod's moment of inertia about its centre is
    m L^2 / 12.
    (a) Find the velocity of the rod's centre and its angular
        velocity just after the strike.
    (b) Find the point of the rod that is momentarily at rest.

3. The ladder that leaves the wall
----------------------------------

    The ladder of problem 1, uniform, of mass m, is released from
    rest with its top 2.4 m up the frictionless wall and its foot
    1.8 m out on the frictionless floor. Its moment of inertia
    about its centre is m L^2 / 12.
    (a) Find its angular velocity as a function of its angle.
    (b) Show that the top leaves the wall when it has come down to
        two thirds of its starting height, and find that height.
    (c) Find the speed of the centre at that moment.

4. The superball
----------------

    A uniform solid superball of mass m and radius R = 2.5 cm,
    moment of inertia 2 m R^2 / 5 about its centre, is thrown
    horizontally with speed v_x, turning at angular velocity omega
    about an axis square to its path. It bounces off a level
    floor without slipping, and elastically: no energy is lost,
    and its vertical speed reverses unchanged.
    (a) Find its horizontal velocity and angular velocity just
        after the bounce, in terms of v_x, omega and R.
    (b) Find them after a second bounce, and describe the motion
        over many bounces.
    (c) Give the numbers for v_x = 2.1 m/s, thrown not turning.

    (This version counts omega positive for forward rolling,
    topspin.)

5. The marble in the trough
---------------------------

    A uniform marble of radius b = 5.0 cm, moment of inertia
    2 m b^2 / 5 about its centre, rolls without slipping inside a
    fixed horizontal cylinder of radius R = 30 cm, across the
    cylinder's axis. Find the period of small oscillations about
    the bottom, and compare it with a bead sliding without
    friction on a circle of radius R - b.
